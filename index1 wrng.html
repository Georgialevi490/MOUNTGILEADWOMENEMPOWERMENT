About Mt Gilead Women Empowerment

Mt Gilead Women Empowerment is a self help group founded and led by Loise Wanjiku Maina. Women come together to build financial discipline, support one another through hard seasons and open doors to new opportunities.

Our meetings are warm, practical and open. Every member has a voice, every contribution is recorded, and every decision is made together.

“When women stand together, no home and no dream is too small to be lifted.”

Loise Wanjiku Maina, Founder

Mission

To help women grow their savings, income and confidence through group effort, training and mutual support.

Vision

Families and communities where women are financially secure, respected and able to lead.

Values

Honesty, accountability, dignity, service to others and working together.

Objectives

Encourage regular saving. Make small loans available through table banking. Support women starting businesses. Train members in practical skills.

Leadership

The group is guided by its founder and an elected team of members. Members take part in decisions and responsibilities are shared.

Membership

Open to women who share the group’s values and are ready to contribute. New members are welcomed through a conversation with the group.

Our activities

Six strands of work run side by side. Each one supports the others.

Table banking

Members meet, contribute to a common fund and take turns borrowing from it at agreed terms. The loans are small, the repayments are public within the group, and the interest stays with the members.

Savings

Regular savings build a cushion for each woman and a base of trust for the whole group. Savings habits learned here carry over into households.

Charity and community support

When a member or neighbour is in need, the group responds with visits, food, prayer and practical help, including time spent with elderly members at home.

Entrepreneurship

Women are helped to turn small ideas into steady businesses, through advice from peers, access to table banking funds and exposure to markets beyond the village, including trade fairs.

Training and skills development

Orientation meetings and workshops cover money management, business basics and new skills. Topics include saving, record keeping and starting a business.

Supporting vulnerable women

Widows, elderly women and women facing hardship are not left on their own. The group looks for ways to walk with them, from encouragement to practical assistance.

Enterprise development

Success stories

Every story in this group begins the same way. A woman comes to her first meeting, makes her first contribution and finds she is not alone. From there a small loan becomes stock, stock becomes a stall, and a stall becomes a steady income. Individual member stories will be shared here in their own words.
